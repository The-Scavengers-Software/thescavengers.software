Cassette by The Scavenger's Software

Privacy Policy

Effective as of 2026-10-18

This privacy policy applies to the Cassette app for Android, Wear OS and iOS (hereby referred to as "Application"), created by The Scavenger's Software LLC (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Open Source service. The Application is an unofficial battery monitor for SRAM AXS bike components and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SRAM. It is provided "AS IS".

In short: the Application has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything it learns about your bikes stays on your devices.

What the Application reads

The Application uses Bluetooth to listen for the broadcasts that AXS components and power meters send when the bike wakes, and connects to them briefly to read their battery levels and system tables. It does this in the background only if you turn background capture on, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

The Application does not use your location. On Android, its Bluetooth permission is declared so that scans are never used to derive location; on iOS, it does not ask for Location access.

What the Application stores

On your phone, the Application stores:

  • the bikes you add: their names, types and optional photos (cropped and resized on the device, never uploaded);
  • each component's identity as the component reports it (serial number, model and firmware), and its battery readings over time (kept for a year);
  • your settings;
  • diagnostic records: raw Bluetooth bytes from your components and from nearby devices that look like SRAM components, so that problems with the reverse-engineered protocol can be investigated.

Your device's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include this data, under the terms of that backup service.

What leaves your phone

  • Your watch. On Android, battery status is sent to your paired Wear OS watch through Google Play services' Wear Data Layer. On iOS, widgets read a copy that stays on the phone.
  • Diagnostics you choose to share. Settings → Diagnostics can export a report. It is sent only when you share it, to whoever you pick in your phone's share sheet.
  • Test builds. Pre-release builds distributed outside the app stores check the Service Provider's update server for newer builds. That check sends the installed build number and the app's identifier, and nothing about your bikes. Builds from Google Play or the App Store do not make this check.

Notifications are scheduled and shown on your device; nothing is sent to a server to produce them.

Third-party services

The Application relies on these services from your device's platform, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services (Wear OS sync, Android only)
  • Apple (iOS widgets and notifications)

The Service Provider does not share or sell your information, because it never receives it. The only exception is a diagnostics report or bug report you choose to send; it is used only to fix the problem it describes.

Deleting your data

Removing a bike in Settings deletes it and its history. Uninstalling the Application deletes everything it stored on your devices. If you have sent a diagnostics report and want it deleted, contact drew@thescavengers.software.

Children

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent the Service Provider personal information, contact drew@thescavengers.software and it will be deleted.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date, and the Application's source history is public at github.com/r0adkll/cassette.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, contact the Service Provider at drew@thescavengers.software.
